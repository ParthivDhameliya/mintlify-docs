## What's included on each plan

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free Forever">
    The Free Forever plan is designed for developers and hobbyists who want to run private AI on their own hardware at zero cost.

    - **1 workspace** — one model, one GPU source, one knowledge base, one API key
    - **1 BYOG device** — connect your own machine via the Neuramine Docker agent
    - **CPU inference** — run models with llama.cpp; no GPU required
    - **2,048-token context** — suitable for concise, focused conversations
    - **2 RAG chunks per request** — lightweight document-grounded answers
    - **3 memories injected** — basic long-term memory per workspace
    - **No credit card required** — you are never charged for GPU time on the Free plan

    <Note>
      The Free plan is BYOG only. Cloud GPU workspaces are not available until you upgrade to Starter or higher.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Starter">
    Starter is built for individual power users who need multiple machines or want to experiment with cloud GPU without a large commitment.

    - **3 workspaces** and **3 BYOG devices**
    - **Cloud GPU access** — spin up serverless cloud GPU that scales to zero when idle
    - **Custom model uploads up to 10 GB** — host your own fine-tuned or quantised models
    - **32,768-token context** — handle longer documents and multi-turn conversations
    - **5 RAG chunks per request** and **10 memories injected**
    - **History sync** — conversation history synced across devices
    - **$10 in trial credits** — credited once to your account on activation

    <Tip>
      Use your $10 trial credits to run a few cloud GPU inference sessions before topping up. Credits cover both the $0.002 flat API fee and any GPU time used.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Max">
    Max is for professionals, researchers, and small teams who need maximum context, larger model files, and guaranteed support response times.

    - **10 workspaces** and **10 BYOG devices**
    - **Cloud GPU access** — same serverless cloud GPU access as Starter
    - **Custom model uploads up to 50 GB** — host large 70B+ quantised models
    - **128,000-token context** — full long-document analysis and extended agentic loops
    - **10 RAG chunks per request** and **20 memories injected**
    - **History sync** and **priority support**
    - **$10 in trial credits** — credited once to your account on activation
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Enterprise">
    Enterprise is for organisations that require compliance, team management, and infrastructure guarantees. See the [Enterprise page](/billing/enterprise) for the full feature list and how to get started.

    - **Unlimited workspaces and BYOG devices**
    - **Dedicated GPU instances** — no shared tenancy, predictable performance
    - **Custom context window** and **unlimited RAG chunks and memories**
    - **SSO / SAML** and full team management
    - **HIPAA BAA and PIPEDA** compliance documentation
    - **Custom SLA**, custom model hosting, and a dedicated account manager
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Trial credits

When you activate a Starter or Max plan for the first time, Neuramine adds **$10 in trial credits** to your account. Use these credits for any inference — cloud GPU time, flat API fees, or both.

<Info>
  Trial credits are granted **once per account lifetime**. They survive plan switches (for example, upgrading from Starter to Max), but are forfeited if you cancel your subscription and later resubscribe.
</Info>

## Upgrading your plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** in the Neuramine dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a new plan">
    Select the plan you want to upgrade to and click **Upgrade**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and pay">
    Review the prorated charge for the remainder of your current billing period and confirm. Your upgrade takes effect **immediately** — new workspace slots and feature limits apply right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you are upgrading from Starter to Max during a trial period, no charge is applied today. The full $99 Max price is billed when your trial ends.
</Note>

## Downgrading your plan

Downgrades are scheduled, not immediate. When you request a downgrade:

- Your current plan's features remain active until the **end of your billing period**.
- On the effective date, any workspaces above the new plan's limit are **suspended** (not deleted). Suspended workspaces retain their data; reactivate them by upgrading again or deleting workspaces to stay within the new limit.
- No partial refunds are issued for unused days on the higher plan.

<Warning>
  Before downgrading to Free Forever, make sure you have no more than one GPU-backed workspace you want to keep. The downgrade flow will ask you to select which workspaces to retain.
</Warning>
