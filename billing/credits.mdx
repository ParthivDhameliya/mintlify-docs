Neuramine uses a prepaid credit balance to cover the cost of AI inference. Every time you send a message to a cloud GPU workspace, two charges are atomically deducted from your balance: a flat API fee and a GPU time fee. Credits never expire, and you can top up at any time from **Settings → Billing** or via the API.

## How charges work

Each inference request triggers exactly two deductions, applied atomically — either both succeed or neither does.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Flat API fee" icon="receipt" href="#how-charges-work">
    **$0.002 per request**

    Applies to every request on paid plans (Starter, Max, Enterprise), regardless of which GPU source the workspace uses — cloud GPU, BYOG, or CPU.
  </Card>
  <Card title="GPU time fee" icon="microchip" href="#how-charges-work">
    **elapsed\_seconds × provider\_rate × 1.30**

    Applies only to **cloud GPU** workspaces. Calculated from the moment inference starts to when the final token is returned, then multiplied by the provider's base rate plus a 30 % platform markup.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
  BYOG (Bring Your Own GPU) and CPU workspaces are **not** charged the GPU time fee. You only pay the $0.002 flat API fee per request. Your own hardware's running costs are yours to manage.
</Note>

### Cloud GPU rates

The GPU time fee is calculated per second of actual inference time, not by the hour. Neuramine routes workloads across cloud GPU infrastructure; the effective rate after the 30 % platform markup ranges from approximately **$1.04 / hr** to **$1.95 / hr** depending on the GPU tier assigned to your workspace. A 3-second response on a standard cloud GPU workspace costs roughly **$0.0012** in GPU time, plus the $0.002 flat fee — about $0.0032 total.

## Credit packages

Purchase credits in preset packages from **Settings → Billing → Add Credits**. Larger packages include a bonus percentage applied instantly at purchase.

| You pay | Credits you receive | Bonus |
|---|---|---|
| $25 | $25.00 | — |
| $50 | $50.00 | — |
| $100 | $100.00 | — |
| $250 | $262.50 | +5 % |
| $500 | $540.00 | +8 % |

<Tip>
  If you run regular workloads, buying the $250 or $500 package gives you more inference time per dollar. The 8 % bonus on a $500 purchase equals $40 of additional credits.
</Tip>

## Trial credits

When you first activate a Starter or Max plan, Neuramine adds **$10 in trial credits** to your account automatically — no purchase required. These credits behave identically to purchased credits and cover both the flat API fee and GPU time charges.

Trial credits are granted **once per account lifetime**. They carry over if you switch plans (e.g. Starter → Max), but are forfeited if you cancel your subscription.

## Auto-recharge

Auto-recharge prevents your workspaces from going offline due to a depleted balance. When your credit balance falls below the threshold you set, Neuramine automatically purchases a credit package on your behalf using your saved payment method.

Configure auto-recharge from **Settings → Billing → Auto-Recharge**:

1. Enable the toggle.
2. Set a **recharge threshold** — the balance level that triggers a top-up (e.g. $5.00).
3. Choose the **package to purchase** when the threshold is hit.

## Monthly spend cap

Set a monthly spend cap to put a hard ceiling on credit consumption for the calendar month. Once your spend reaches the cap, inference requests are paused until the next month begins or you raise the cap.

- Minimum cap: **$5.00**
- Maximum cap: **$10,000**
- Set to **$0** to remove the cap entirely.

Configure your cap from **Settings → Billing → Spend Cap** or via the API.

<Warning>
  The spend cap counts credits consumed by inference only. Manual credit purchases and auto-recharge purchases do not count toward the cap, and credits purchased above the cap remain in your balance for future months.
</Warning>

## Checking your balance

### In the dashboard

Navigate to **Settings → Billing**. Your current credit balance, active tier, auto-recharge status, and monthly spend cap are displayed at the top of the page.

### Via the API

Use the `GET /v1/billing/balance` endpoint to retrieve your balance programmatically. This is useful for building spend-monitoring scripts or internal dashboards.

<CodeGroup>
```bash cURL
curl https://api.neuramine.ai/v1/billing/balance \
  -H 'Authorization: Bearer nrm_sk_...'
```

```python Python
import requests

resp = requests.get(
    "https://api.neuramine.ai/v1/billing/balance",
    headers={"Authorization": "Bearer nrm_sk_..."},
)
print(resp.json())
```
</CodeGroup>

**Example response:**

```json
{
  "credit_balance_usd": 47.38,
  "tier": "starter",
  "pending_tier": null,
  "api_request_fee_usd": 0.002,

  "packages": [
    { "usd": 25,  "bonus_pct": 0, "credit_usd": 25.0 },
```
