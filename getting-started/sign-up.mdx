Creating a Neuramine account takes under a minute. Go to [cloud.neuramine.ai](https://cloud.neuramine.ai) and choose the sign-up method that suits you — no password and no credit card are required to get started.

## Choose a sign-up method

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Google" icon="google" href="https://cloud.neuramine.ai">
    Click **Continue with Google** to sign in instantly using your existing Google account via OAuth. No form to fill in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Magic Link Email" icon="envelope" href="https://cloud.neuramine.ai">
    Enter your email address and click **Send Magic Link**. Open the email and click the link to sign in — no password needed, ever.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Tip>
  Magic links expire after 15 minutes. If yours has expired, return to [cloud.neuramine.ai](https://cloud.neuramine.ai) and request a new one.
</Tip>

## What you get on the Free Forever plan

The Free Forever plan is active immediately after sign-up and never expires. It gives you everything you need to get started with BYOG on your own hardware.

| Feature | Free Forever | Starter ($19/mo) | Max ($99/mo) | Enterprise |
|---------|:------------:|:----------------:|:------------:|:----------:|
| Workspaces | 1 | 3 | 10 | Unlimited |
| BYOG devices | 1 | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Cloud GPU | — | ✓ | ✓ | Dedicated |
| Model registry access | Public models | Public models | Public models | Custom models |
| History sync | — | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| SSO / SAML | — | — | — | ✓ |
| BAA / DPA (HIPAA) | — | — | — | ✓ |
| API fee per request | Free | $0.002 | $0.002 | Custom |

<Info>
  Starter and Max plans include **$10 in trial credits** valid for 7 days from the first upgrade. Trial credits do not carry over if you cancel your plan.
</Info>

## Upgrade your plan

You can upgrade at any time from **Settings → Billing** inside the dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately — you don't need to recreate your workspaces or re-register your devices.

<Note>
  If you're on Free Forever and need more than one workspace or want to use cloud GPU inference, upgrade to Starter to unlock those features.
</Note>
