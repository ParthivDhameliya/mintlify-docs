## How to get started

Follow these four steps to go from a new account to a running AI workspace.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up">
    Create a free account at [cloud.neuramine.ai](https://cloud.neuramine.ai) using Google OAuth or a magic link sent to your email. No credit card required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a Workspace">
    Click **New Workspace** in the dashboard, give it a name, and choose a model from the registry. Each workspace gets its own API key and conversation history.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the Agent">
    Download the Neuramine agent for your platform and start it with your device token. The agent connects outbound only — no firewall changes needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Chatting">
    Once your device shows **online** in the dashboard, open the chat interface or call the API. All inference runs on your hardware.
  </Step>
</Steps>
